⚽ Origins of Soccer

Soccer began over 2,000 years ago in ancient China with a game called Cuju. Players kicked a ball into a net without using their hands. Similar games also appeared in Greece and Rome.

The modern version of soccer was created in England in 1863 when official rules were written. This transformed the sport into what we know today.

⚽ Rise of Modern Football

ABSOLUTE BOX

During the late 1800s, soccer spread across Europe and South America. Clubs formed and competitions grew rapidly.

FIFA was founded in 1904 and now organizes the World Cup, the biggest sporting event in the world.

⚽ Soccer Legends

Soccer has produced legendary players like Pelé, Diego Maradona, and Lionel Messi, who changed the game forever.

Their skills, achievements, and passion helped make soccer the most popular sport on Earth.

⚽ Biggest Competitions

The FIFA World Cup started in 1930 and is held every four years. It is the most watched sporting event globally.

Other major competitions include the UEFA Champions League, Premier League, and La Liga.
