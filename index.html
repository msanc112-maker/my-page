History of Soccer

From ancient origins to the world's most popular sport

Origins of Soccer

Soccer, known as football in most parts of the world, has a history that dates back more than 2,000 years. Early versions of the game were played in ancient civilizations such as China, Greece, and Rome. One of the earliest recorded forms was a Chinese game called Cuju, where players kicked a ball into a net without using their hands.

Modern Development

The modern version of soccer began in England during the 19th century. Before this time, different regions played their own versions of the game with different rules. In 1863, the Football Association was formed, creating the first official rules and establishing the foundation of modern soccer.

Global Expansion

Soccer quickly spread across Europe and the rest of the world. In 1904, FIFA was founded to organize international competitions and govern the sport. The first FIFA World Cup took place in 1930 in Uruguay, marking a major moment in soccer history.

Soccer Today

Today, soccer is the most popular sport in the world, played and watched by billions of people. It continues to grow with professional leagues, international tournaments, and technological advancements, while still maintaining the simplicity that made it famous.
